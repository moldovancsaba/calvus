4. What the others do well that business.direct should keep

  • Holdvölgy's generator (one content source → HU and EN, every page) and its per-page build logs are the model for any multi-page, multi-language site; business.direct is one page in one language by directive and needs neither.
  • IDBC's data converters with the client's workbooks as arguments, output verified byte-identical on a re-run — business.direct's converter follows the same discipline (the re-pull on 2026-09-19 was diffed record by record).
  • DiscountDirect's decided stack shows what the ADRs become once a client says yes.
  • Lexodont's honesty ("no research was done for this project") is the right way to record an absence.
