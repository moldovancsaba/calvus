## Patterns

1. **Ground: 13 light, 1 dark** (VIK). Accent lives in the photography.
2. **Type: sans body in every case that could be read.** Serif appears as wordmark
   (Jordan, Royal Tokaji), signature (Opus One) or italic punctuation (Krug, Pasqua).
3. **Three commercial models**: no transaction (first growths, Royal Tokaji,
   Disznókő); allocation/membership gate (Opus One, Stag's Leap, VIK, Jordan);
   open DTC with prices (Penfolds, Garzón, Barta, Klein Constantia). Holdvölgy is
   in the third with a membership layer — see D9.
4. **Navigation ≤ 7 items**, always some ordering of Estate · Wines · Visit ·
   Club · Contact.
5. **Hero = photograph + one sentence.** Never a paragraph, never four heroes.
6. **Named vineyards as objects**; **a founding number**; **vintage as narrative**;
   **provenance devices** (Krug iD, VIK Authentication — Holdvölgy's Trezor
   certificate and Time Capsule are the same idea).
7. **Hospitality CTA before shop CTA** everywhere.
