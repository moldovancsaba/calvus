# Phase 2 — the Birtok (Estate) page, measured

Built 2026-09-16, HU `birtok.html` and EN `en/birtok.html`, generated by
`holdvolgy/build.py` from the same content dictionary as the home page. Every
sentence on it is the estate's own, taken from the live `/birtok` page
(`02-brand-and-site-audit.md`): the vision statement and the founder's quote, the
ten timeline steps 1998–2019 with their texts, all seven dűlők with planting year,
exposure, rock and varieties, the six varieties, the five vintage notes 2021–2025,
and the eight team members with role and quote.

## Sections

Hero (the vineyard photograph — a 5760 px original, the best on the site — with
"A gondolatok bora"); Vízió with the founder's quote and small portrait; Történet
as ten illustrated cards (rows on phone, 2-up tablet, 5-up desktop); Dűlők és
terroir with the map and seven cards each carrying rock photo, Telepítés,
Kitettség, Kőzet, Fajták (Úrágya and Kakasok show the marked gap); Szőlőfajták;
Évjáratok as native `<details>`, 2025 open; Csapat as eight cards with a note that
portraits are to come; a visit call-to-action.

Rock photographs use `srcset` (256 / 640) with `sizes`, so phones take the small
files and desktop the gallery size.
