# Lexodont — research

*Written 2026-09-18. Honest scope: no research was done for this project.*

## What was not done

No benchmark of dental-practice websites, no competitor read, no audit of the client's
previous site, no user research. The wireframe was built from the client's brief and
feedback in two working days (2026-03-31, 2026-04-09), as a structural sketch to agree
page set, navigation, sections and calls to action. That was its job and it was
sufficient for it: the built site follows the agreed structure (`02-audit.md`).

## What was read

- The client's brief and feedback as they arrived (not archived in this repo; the commit
  messages of 2026-03-31 and 2026-04-09 are the only trace — `04-decisions.md`).
- The practice's real contact, hours, parking and transport information, which the
  wireframe carries verbatim.
- Partner names (Straumann, Invisalign, 3Shape, Ivoclar, EMS AirFlow, GC Europe,
  Coltene) — from the client.

## The one measurement, after the fact

The live lexodont.hu was measured on 2026-09-18 for this package (`02-audit.md`,
`11-architecture.md` §2). It is the only external evidence in the project.

## If Lexodont becomes a client again

A second iteration would deserve: a read of five to ten Hungarian and international
private-practice sites (booking flow, price presentation, before/after ethics, team
pages), a look at the practice's analytics (GA4 is installed), and the questions in
`08-client-asks.md`.
