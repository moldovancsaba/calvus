Layout

  • Navbar fixed, flush to the top, overlaying the hero (from the SAP mockup); hamburger below 980 px; five items + two right-aligned actions (D19).
  • Hero with a dark card bottom-left; eyebrow, h1, one sentence.
  • Summary block of three green cards above every chart (band per perspective; on Expert Pool: pool size / positions / largest pool).
  • TOP3 chart: wide layout above 700 px (role names in a left gutter, one shared axis), compact layout below (one block per position with its own scale; D18); LinkedIn pill under the role name; footnote under the chart (D22).
  • Tables: desktop table; cards below 600 px of container width (D17); Bérek groups collapse behind a header showing the full band.
  • Area page: summary text clamped to the media slot's height with "Olvass tovább"; media slot = video placeholder or green key-thought box (D20).
  • Expert Community: intro → two audience cards (join form behind a <details>, Ajánlatkérés anchor) → tiles (bar block anchored to the card bottom, D26) → contact form.
  • Articles (SAP trends, case studies): one column, ≤ 760 px measure (D25).

375 px and desktop on every page after each change round since 2026-09-15; the numbers are in the change notes in SOURCES-AND-GAPS.md and summarised in 07-gate.md.
