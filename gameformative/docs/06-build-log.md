# Build log

*Every build round: what changed, what was measured, what was fixed. Newest last.*

## Round 1 — 2026-09-25 — the first version

**Data.** `data/fetch.py` downloaded nine openfootball files (CC0). The first run of
`data/convert.py` passed the five 2026/27 leagues and the 2025/26 Premier League, then **stopped on
the World Cup**: its two files spell four nations differently ("Côte d'Ivoire" / "Ivory Coast",
"Cabo Verde" / "Cape Verde", "Congo DR" / "DR Congo", "Bosnia and Herzegovina" / "Bosnia &
Herzegovina"). Fixed with an explicit alias table in `data/teams.py`; the match-by-match cross-check
then passed. The 2026/27 files also write some results as a bare `[h, a]` list instead of
`{"ft": [h, a]}` — read as results, documented in `12-technical-design.md` §3.

**Copy.** Five articles written in `content.py`, every figure from `stats.json`. Three sentences
were rewritten before any page was built because the data could not back them: a general
"title form is about two points a game" rule of thumb (replaced by last season's champion's
actual PPG), "the pattern is familiar from earlier seasons" (no earlier season in the data; replaced
by the added-time share), and an "October international break" (the dataset shows a fixture gap,
not its reason; replaced by the date the schedule resumes). Premises added for every narrative
claim (D7).

**Pages.** 19 pages generated by `build.py`: home, scores, stats hub, five league pages, the
2025/26 season review, World Cup 2026 and its final, the analysis index and five pieces, How we
count, the style guide.
