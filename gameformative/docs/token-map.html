Token map — prototype to production

Every token and component the prototype defines, and what it becomes in the real build. Written 2026-09-25; updated 2026-09-26 (the results strip, league snapshot and article key-facts box were removed from the prototype and from this map). The prototype's names (--gf-*, .gf-*) are the production names: nothing is renamed, so a developer can lift tokens.css as the design-token file.
