## Why this matters now

The hook: the finding, the moment, the question. Second person is welcome ("Your GPS watch says…").

## The landscape

What is known, who measured it, and how — in plain words. Numbers with their units and their source.

## What you can do with this

The practical turn: what a coach, athlete, fan or buyer should do, check or ask.

## What we would keep

The part of the practice or the claim that holds up.

## What we would question

Where the evidence is thin, contested, or being oversold.

## How the field is arguing it

The strongest case on each side, fairly.

## Closing note

One line to remember, and the open question worth caring about.
