Points per game, goal difference and form: how to read a table in September

Leagues that have played different numbers of rounds, teams with a game in hand, a run of five results — three tools for reading a table before it settles.

By gameformative data desk25 September 20261 min read5 segments

In this article

  1. The problem
  2. Points per game
  3. Goal difference and tie-breakers
  4. Form
  5. What the table cannot tell you

The problem

In late September the tables of Europe’s big five leagues are not comparable at a glance. LaLiga has played 7 rounds, the Bundesliga 4. Three simple measures help.

Points per game

Points divided by matches played. It puts a team with a game in hand level with everyone else, and lets you compare across leagues: Barcelona currently lead Europe’s big five on 3.00 points per game. Last season Arsenal won the Premier League on 2.24.

Goal difference and tie-breakers

Goals scored minus goals conceded. On gameformative, teams level on points are ordered by goal difference, then goals scored, then name — the site’s own rule, stated on every table. Official rules differ: LaLiga and Serie A look first at the matches between the teams level on points, and Serie A settles a tie for the title or for relegation with a play-off. For teams level on points, an official table can differ from ours.

Form

The last five results, oldest on the left, most recent on the right. We print the letter — W, D, L — in every chip and use colour only to repeat it, so the guide reads the same in black and white and for readers with colour-vision deficiency.

What the table cannot tell you

Our data carries results, goals, line-ups and attendances. It does not carry shots, possession or expected goals (xG); those need a licensed event-data provider. Where a figure would need that data, the page says so instead of estimating it.

Sources

Sources used

  2. Criterios de desempate en LaLiga — Flashscore EspañaLaLiga’s order for teams level on points: the matches between them first.
  3. Serie A: chi vince a pari punti e in caso di classifica avulsa — DAZN ItaliaSerie A’s head-to-head criteria and the play-off (spareggio).
