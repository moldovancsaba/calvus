One goal in four came after the 75th minute: the 2025/26 Premier League by the clock

270 of 1,045 goals arrived in the last quarter-hour of normal time, more than twice as many as in the first fifteen minutes.

By gameformative data desk25 September 20261 min read3 segments

In this article

  1. By the clock
  2. Added time
  3. Who scored them

By the clock

Across the 380 matches of the 2025/26 Premier League, 1,045 goals were scored. Split into six periods of fifteen minutes, the distribution is anything but flat.

The last quarter-hour of normal time, including added time, produced 270 goals — 25.8% of the season’s total, against 121 in the opening fifteen minutes. The period just before half-time, including first-half added time, was second with 216.

Added time

Added time alone accounts for 147 goals across both halves — 14.1% of the season’s total, scored in minutes that are not on the regulation clock. 77 goals were penalties and 40 were own goals.

Who scored them

Erling Haaland finished as the league’s top scorer with 27, 5 ahead of Igor Thiago, whose 22 included 8 penalties. Arsenal won the title with 85 points.
