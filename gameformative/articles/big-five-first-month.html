Europe’s big five after a month: the Bundesliga scores most, the Premier League draws most

3.81 goals a game in Germany, 2.82 in England — and not a single goalless draw yet in Serie A.

By gameformative data desk25 September 20261 min read3 segments

In this article

  1. Where the goals are
  2. Who wins where
  3. Two oddities

Where the goals are

A month into the season, Europe’s five biggest leagues have produced 761 goals in 250 matches. They have not produced them evenly.

The Bundesliga leads at 3.81 goals a game, helped by results such as FCB 7–0 FCU. The Premier League is lowest at 2.82. The samples are small and uneven: between 36 and 69 matches per league, after 4 to 7 rounds.

Who wins where

Home advantage looks strongest in the Bundesliga, where the home side has won 52.8% of matches. The Premier League has the most draws, 32% — 16 of 50.

Two oddities

Serie A has not had a single 0–0 in 50 matches, and both teams have scored in 60% of Ligue 1 games, the highest share of the five.

The published schedule resumes on 9 October; we will re-run these numbers when every league has at least seven rounds behind it.
