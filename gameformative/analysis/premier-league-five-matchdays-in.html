Five from five: what Man City’s perfect start does — and doesn’t — tell us

15 points from 5 matches is the best start in the league. Last season, the team with 15 after five finished fifth.

By gameformative data desk25 September 2026Data to 20 September 2026

Key numbers

  • Man City: 5 wins from 5, 13 scored, 5 conceded.
  • Brighton have the most goals (16) and the biggest win of the season so far.
  • 2025/26: Liverpool had 15 points after five and finished fifth; Arsenal, the champions, had 10.

Man City have won all 5 of their Premier League matches, scoring 13 and conceding 5. That is 15 points, the maximum available, and 3 clear of Arsenal in second.

The league’s most productive attack belongs to someone else. Brighton have scored 16 — the most of any side — and recorded the biggest win of the season so far, 0–5 at COV on 13 September.

Why five matches is not a verdict

Last season’s table is the warning. After five matchdays of 2025/26, Liverpool had 15 points — more than anyone — and finished fifth on 60. Arsenal, who won the title with 85, had 10 at the same stage.

The league as a whole is also running hot on a small sample. 50 matches in, it has averaged 2.82 goals a game, against 2.75 across all 380 of last season, and 32% of matches have been drawn, against 27.4% over the full campaign.

At the other end, Fulham and Tottenham have 2 and 2 points and are still looking for a first win. The next round is scheduled for 10 October.
