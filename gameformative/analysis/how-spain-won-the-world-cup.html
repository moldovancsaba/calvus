How Spain won the 2026 World Cup: 14 scored, 1 conceded

Eight matches, one goal against, and a final settled in extra time by a substitute. The tournament in numbers.

By gameformative data desk25 September 2026Data to 20 September 2026

Key numbers

  • Spain: 8 matches, 14 goals scored, 1 conceded.
  • Final: Spain 1–0 Argentina after extra time; Ferran Torres 106′, on as a substitute in the 62th minute.
  • Tournament: 308 goals in 104 matches (2.96 a game); top scorer Kylian Mbappe (France), 10.

Spain’s World Cup began with a 0–0 against Cape Verde and ended with a 1–0 against Argentina. In between they scored 14 goals and conceded 1, in eight matches.

The final in New Jersey, in front of 80,663, was goalless after 90 minutes. Ferran Torres, who had come on in the 62th minute, scored in the 106th.

The tournament around them

The first 48-team World Cup produced 308 goals in 104 matches, 2.96 a game. 9 matches went to extra time and 4 were settled on penalties.

The golden boot went to Kylian Mbappe of France with 10, whose team lost to Spain in the semi-final and then took part in the tournament’s highest-scoring match: France 4–6 England in the match for third place.

The most productive quarter-hour of the tournament was the last one: 81 goals came between the 76th minute and the end of normal time.
