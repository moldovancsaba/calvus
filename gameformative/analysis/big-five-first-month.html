Europe’s big five after a month: the Bundesliga scores most, the Premier League draws most

3.81 goals a game in Germany, 2.82 in England — and not a single goalless draw yet in Serie A.

By gameformative data desk25 September 2026Data to 20 September 2026

Key numbers

  • Goals per game: Bundesliga 3.81, LaLiga 3.04, Serie A 2.90, Ligue 1 2.84, Premier League 2.82.
  • Home wins are most common in the Bundesliga (52.8% of matches).
  • Serie A: 0 goalless draws in 50 matches.

A month into the season, Europe’s five biggest leagues have produced 761 goals in 250 matches. They have not produced them evenly.

The Bundesliga leads at 3.81 goals a game, helped by results such as FCB 7–0 FCU. The Premier League is lowest at 2.82. The gap is real in this sample, but the samples are small and uneven: between 36 and 69 matches per league, because the leagues have played between 4 and 7 rounds.

Who wins where

Home advantage looks strongest in the Bundesliga, where the home side has won 52.8% of matches. The Premier League has the most draws, 32% — 16 of 50.

Two smaller oddities: Serie A has not had a single 0–0 in 50 matches, and both teams have scored in 60% of Ligue 1 games, the highest share of the five.

The published schedule resumes on 9 October; we will re-run these numbers when every league has at least seven rounds behind it.
