Bízd ránk a zöldet — brief

Stage 0 of the prototyping standard (PROTOTYPING.md). For the owner and the team. What this document holds: who the client is, what they sell, what the brief asks, what the prototype covers, and what is real. Written 2026-09-20 from what the owner said and what the client's own site, Instagram and parent brand expose.

The client

Bízd ránk a zöldet! ("Leave the green to us", bizdrankazoldet.hu, Instagram @bizdrankazoldet) — a Hungarian B2B service brand for office plants: design, installation, plant rental (növénybérlés) and plant care (növénygondozás) for offices, with maintenance as part of the service, not an option. Legal entity Gourmet Garden Kft. (Budapest), which also trades as Grofie (grofiezoldfal.com): green walls, indoor greening and garden construction, "100+ projects, 10+ years, 30+ maintenance sites, 100 % evergreen guarantee", same phone number, IVSZ member, covered by Portfolio.hu in 2020 for a 57 m² green wall in a WELL-certified office. Bízd ránk a zöldet is the new, playful office-plant brand of an established company — the site's images date from April 2026, the Instagram account from September 2026 (70 followers, four Reels and a post in its first three weeks).

What they sell, in their words

Office plants "for people who have already killed a cactus": the office is not a rainforest; plants are chosen for the office's light, air and rhythm, not the catalogue; the whole project — survey, design, installation, care — is one hand; "don't let the intern water them"; a well-planned green office beats a foosball table. Three offers: office plant decoration, plant care, plant rental (also for events). One call to action: send photos of your office, get a proposal.

The brief

"Make the full research on how a silly but professional green project should be presented in 2026 to give trust, reliability and a strong identity and a customer journey that sells." The research is 01-research.md; the client's current presence measured is 02-audit.md; what is real and what is sample is 03-sources.md. The prototype follows the research through the standard's stages: design system (gate 1), layout frames (gate 2), build, sweep, presentation in Hungarian (bemutato.html, the client's language).

What the prototype covers

A prototype of the brand's public presence that sells to an office decision-maker (office manager, HR, founder, facility lead): the home page, the three service pages, a references / proof page, a "how it works and what it costs from" page, the quote flow with photo upload, and the Instagram-facing identity (the character, the tone, the content formats) — in Hungarian, mobile first (the decision-maker sees Instagram on a phone and the site from a link).

What is real

The client's site copy, structure and measurements (02-audit.md), the Instagram profile's public facts, the parent brand's published numbers and guarantee, the competitors' public pages, and every research figure with its source. Sample until the client provides it: photos of real installations, client names for references, prices, the team's names and faces, Google review count.

Where it stands

Stage 0 (this brief), stage 1 (research and audit) and stage 2 (sources) written 2026-09-20. Next: the owner reads the research and says "direction approved"; then the design system.
