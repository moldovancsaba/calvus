the best case studies and best practices as a source of our services" — *it* being the

- **Yelp Assistant** project submissions +400 % year over year in Q2 2025; the business

  [Yelp spring 2025 release](https://blog.yelp.com/news/spring-product-release-2025/)

- **Hatch acquisition, 21 January 2026, $270 million cash + $30 million retention**: an
  AI lead-management and customer-communication platform, $25 million ARR growing 70 %;
  Yelp's words — Hatch is "solving challenging lead management and communication pain points
  for services businesses" to "help service providers operate and grow more efficiently".
  **P** [Yelp IR](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2026/Yelp-Accelerates-Strategy-with-Acquisition-of-AI-Lead-Management-Platform-Hatch/default.aspx).
  Hatch's predictive lead scoring lets media buying reallocate budget in real time toward

## 2. Unit-economics best practice (the rules behind the economics screen)

- **LTV : CAC of 3 : 1 is the accepted health line**; below 1 : 1 is unsustainable, above
  5 : 1 signals under-investment. Sales-led SMB companies target 6–12-month payback at
  3 : 1. **A** [PM Toolkit](https://pmtoolkit.ai/benchmarks/ltv-cac-ratio-benchmarks),
  [SaaS Hero](https://www.saashero.net/strategy/b2b-saas-ltv-cac-benchmarks/)
- **CAC payback**: median B2B SaaS 15–16 months; top quartile 6–8; bottom quartile 24–36;
  Bessemer's targets by segment — **under 12 months for SMB**, 18 mid-market, 24 enterprise.
  **A** [Aleph](https://www.getaleph.com/answers/cac-payback-period-saas-2026),
  [Optifai, 939 companies](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/cac-payback-period-benchmark/),
  [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/cac-payback-benchmarks-2026/)
- **The metrics set**: a16z's 16 startup metrics (CAC, LTV, ARPA, churn, activation,
  retention …) and 13 marketplace metrics (GMV, take rate, liquidity, supply/demand
  concentration). **P** [a16z: 16 startup metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/),
  [a16z: 13 marketplace metrics](https://a16z.com/13-metrics-for-marketplace-companies/),
  [16 more](https://a16z.com/16-more-startup-metrics/); **A** [Lenny's marketplace metrics](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-most-important-marketplace-metrics)
- **Cohorts, not averages**: LTV and churn by acquisition month and by channel, so that a
  CAC is compared with the LTV of the cohort it bought. **A** [DualEntry](https://www.dualentry.com/blog/saas-unit-economics)

**Reading.** The economics screen's 3 : 1 badge and the 12-month payback line are the
industry's lines, not ours; the plan's next step is cohorts by acquisition channel
(touch · call · content) once events exist.

## 3. Measurement best practice: attribution, incrementality, mix modelling

- **Triangulate three methods**: attribution for the day-to-day, incrementality tests for
  causal proof, marketing-mix modelling for the budget. **A** [Measured](https://www.measured.com/faq/marketing-mix-modeling-2026-complete-guide-for-strategic-marketers/),
  [FELD M](https://www.feld-m.de/en/blog/google-meridian-triangulation-future-marketing-measurement/)
- **Open-source MMM exists and is free**: Google **Meridian** (Bayesian, with **GeoX**
  geo-incrementality announced May 2026), Meta **Robyn**, PyMC-Marketing. **P** [Google Meridian](https://business.google.com/uk/think/measurement/meridian-marketing-mix-model/);
  **A** [Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/marketing-mix-modeling-providers),
  [Elevarus on GeoX](https://elevarus.com/marketing-mix-modeling-meridian-google-marketing-live-2026/)
- **But not yet for us**: under ~2 years of data or under ~$1 million annual spend the
  sample is too small for stable coefficients — start with last-touch attribution plus
  periodic incrementality tests (geo holdouts, PSA controls) and revisit MMM in 12–18
  months. **A** [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/marketing-mix-modeling-2026-mmm-vs-attribution-playbook)
- **Marketplace experiments interfere with themselves** (a treated listing takes demand
  from a control listing); Airbnb's answer is cluster randomisation — by neighbourhood, not
  by listing. **P** [Management Science, Airbnb pricing meta-experiment](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.01157),
  [Airbnb engineering: Experiments at Airbnb](https://medium.com/airbnb-engineering/experiments-at-airbnb-e2db3abf39e7)

**Reading.** Our attribution (last touch, 7 days for a sign-up, 30 for a booking —
analytics §6) is the right first stage. The first *incrementality* test business.direct
can run is a **neighbourhood holdout**: run the content engine in Park Slope and not in
Carroll Gardens for four weeks and compare sign-ups — cluster by neighbourhood exactly as
Airbnb does. MMM waits.

## 4. Decision best practice: experimentation and next-best-action

- **Booking.com runs ~25,000 experiments a year**; anyone can test without permission;
  the culture, not the tooling, is the asset (Stefan Thomke, HBS). **P** [HBR, March 2020](https://hbr.org/2020/03/building-a-culture-of-experimentation),
  [HBR IdeaCast 2024](https://hbr.org/podcast/2024/06/how-booking-com-sustains-a-culture-of-innovation);
  **A** [VWO](https://vwo.com/blog/cro-best-practices-booking/)
- **Next-best-action**: lifecycle teams replace segment campaigns with a per-customer
  decision (which action, which channel, which moment) driven by propensity scores
  (convert, churn, upgrade) and continuous 1 : 1 experiments. **A** [Hightouch](https://hightouch.com/blog/next-best-action),
  [Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/next-best-action),
  [CleverTap](https://clevertap.com/blog/next-best-action/)
- **Lead scoring reallocates spend**: Hatch's model (now Yelp's) scores each lead and
  moves budget toward the channels that produced high scores. **A** [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/yelp-bets-270m-on-ai-with-hatch-acquisition-to-automate-services-leads/)

**Reading.** Our "next dollar" (analytics §4) is next-best-action at the *budget* level;
the provider-level version is a **propensity to claim / to upgrade** per provider (phone
on card, trial policy, next session, replies) that orders the sequence and the call list —
one score, computed nightly from the same events.

## 5. What the case studies say about the provider's own numbers

- Speed to lead, reminders and reply time (research I §2–3) are the numbers Yelp now sells
  back to the provider as Receptionist and Hatch; Angi prices leads by quality.
- A provider's dashboard therefore needs three lines, in this order: **enquiries answered
  and how fast**, **trials → enrolments and their value**, **what the plan costs** — the
  "your return" line built in round 5.

## 8. What was not found

- Yelp and Angi do not disclose CAC or advertiser churn; revenue per location / per lead
  and paying-location counts are the public proxies.
- Rover's "Perfect Match" and LTV figures come from secondary write-ups, not a filing.
- No public case study of a *youth-activities* platform's unit economics exists; the
  home-services comparables are the nearest.
- **Link check** (`curl`, 2026-09-19): eight sources refuse scripted fetches (SEC EDGAR
  requires a declared user agent; Yelp IR, PPC Land, Medium, INFORMS and an Angi PDF return
  403 / no response) — each was read through the fetch tool at writing time and is kept as
  the primary source; open them in a browser.
