business.direct — research

What the evidence says about the four automations and the one-person machine, with the source behind every figure. Searched 2026-09-19. Primary sources (studies, platform disclosures, regulators) are marked P; industry aggregators are marked A and carry less weight — several repeat each other and cite older data. Nothing here was measured by us except the two reference platforms (02-audit.md).

1. The listed business that does nothing loses to the one that does

  • A one-star increase in a Yelp rating raises an independent restaurant's revenue by 5–9 %; chains see almost no effect — reputation matters most where nobody knows the brand. P Luca, HBS working paper, Harvard Magazine summary
  • Hotels that start responding to reviews receive 12 % more reviews and their rating rises 0.12 stars — because unhappy guests post fewer baseless complaints when they see the business answers. P HBR / INFORMS study, INFORMS release
  • Businesses with photos on their Google profile get 42 % more direction requests and 35 % more website clicks (Google's own figure, widely quoted); profiles with 100+ photos get several times more calls and clicks (BrightLocal, 15 191 listings). A BrightLocal / Google via re:spondelligent, thestacc
  • Claimed and complete profiles: claimed Google listings are quoted at +37 % local visibility; complete profiles at 2.7× more likely to be seen as reputable, 70 % more visits; about 41 % of small local businesses still run incomplete profiles. A newmedia, sqmagazine, LocaliQ review stats
  • 88 % of consumers who search locally on a phone visit or call within 24 hours (Think with Google, quoted); "near me" searches have grown more than 900 % since 2018. A BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2025 (1 026 US adults), via K2Z

Reading for business.direct. The activation automation is not a courtesy: an unclaimed, photo-less, unanswered card is measurably worth less to the business than a claimed one, and the platform can show the business exactly that.

2. Speed: the machine answers first

  • MIT / InsideSales lead-response study (2007; 6 companies, 15 000+ leads, 100 000+ call attempts): the odds of qualifying a lead are 21× higher when contacted within 5 minutes than after 30; the odds of contacting at all drop 100× between 5 and 30 minutes. P the study PDF
  • HBR (2.24 million leads): firms that respond within an hour are ~7× more likely to qualify the lead than those waiting an hour more; waiting 24 hours makes it 60× less likely. P via Kixie, LeadAngel
  • 62 % of calls to small businesses go unanswered (411 Locals, 2016: 85 businesses, 58 industries, 30 days: 37.8 % answered, 37.8 % voicemail, 24.3 % no response); 85 % of missed callers do not call back; automated missed-call text-back is quoted to recover most of them. A SkipCalls on the 411 Locals study, getaira
  • 78 % of customers buy from the business that responds first; SMS open rates ~98 %. A SchedulingKit

Reading. "Someone tags you — first, not a queue" (video 1) has numbers behind it. Reviews, messages, missed calls and booking requests are the department the business sees value from in the first week.

3. Reminders and rhythm: the cheapest revenue

  • A review of 29 studies: automated reminders improve non-attendance by 29 %; studios report no-shows falling from 20–30 % to 8–12 % with SMS sequences; a reminder three days and one day before beats a single one. P/A Glofox on the PubMed review, DailyStory
  • Trial-to-member conversion is quoted to improve 15–20 points when the first reply arrives within 5 minutes. A US Tech Automations
  • The European fitness market: 75.5 million members at end-2025 (+4.1 million), revenue €39.1 bn (+9.1 %) — the category the sport platform lists most (148 gyms of 431) is growing. A Athletech on the EuropeActive report

Reading. DiscountDirect's research already established the replenishment-reminder lever for products; for listed businesses the equivalent is the session reminder and the "this week near you" rhythm.

4. Digests and alerts to consumers

  • Email benchmarks 2025: open rates 35–45 % by industry (inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection — click rate is the honest metric, 1.9–3.4 %); location-specific subject lines gave 18 % more opens. A/P Mailchimp benchmarks, HubSpot, MailerLite
  • App push opt-in: ~61 % overall (Android 67 %, iOS 56 %, falling); web push 5–10 %. A Business of Apps, MobiLoud, Airship benchmark PDF
  • Local intent exists: 71 % of adults want to meet more people in their neighbourhood (Harris Poll for Nextdoor); 54 % are more likely to attend a locally trending event. A Nextdoor / Harris, Eventbrite statistics
  • Frequency: DiscountDirect's research (already in this repo) found four messages a month is where most people unsubscribe — the cap applies here unchanged.

Reading. One weekly digest per consumer, built from saved items, location, age band and the week's sessions, with click rate as the metric — not opens.

5. Generated pages: how listing sites earn search traffic

  • Zillow: ~5.2 million indexed pages, the vast majority generated per city, neighbourhood and property type; Tripadvisor's programmatic pages carry ~226 million organic visits a month; the pattern is one page tree: country › region › city › neighbourhood › category. A GrackerAI on Zillow, Practical Programmatic on Tripadvisor, Backlinko
  • The sport platform already has the tree: 8 categories × 20 counties × 147 localities × 7 age bands, with counts per facet from /api/public/browse-facets (02-audit.md).

Reading. The generated-pages automation is the platform's own SEO department; every page must have real listings behind it (no empty category × place pages — a thin-content risk the audit's facet counts prevent).

6. How listing platforms monetise the business side

  • Indeed: sponsored jobs get up to 5× more clicks and are 65 % more likely to report a hire than free posts; three free posts a month direct on Indeed. P Indeed: free vs sponsored, Indeed pricing
  • Mindbody marketplace: pay 20 % (capped at $30) only on the first purchase of a new client the app delivered; intro offers get premium placement. P Mindbody app listing
  • Booksy: subscription including messaging, free marketplace listing, a paid "Boost" for visibility. P Booksy pricing. Treatwell: 10–20 % commission on completed bookings plus premium listings. A Sharetribe
  • Yelp: claiming is free and separate from advertising; the free tools are answering reviews and messages and seeing views and leads. P Yelp: what is a claimed business, Yelp for Business

Reading. The activation ladder every marketplace uses: free claim → free tools that show value (reviews, messages, views) → paid visibility or pay-per-new-customer. business.direct's platform view should show the ladder as states of a listing.

7. AI in the small business's marketing — adoption and the human in the loop

  • Adoption: 54 % of US small businesses use AI marketing tools, 27 % more plan to (US Chamber, Aug 2025); other 2026 surveys quote 73–87 %; primary uses content creation (48 %), customer-service automation (42 %), lead scoring (31 %). A Capsule, Forbes, theStacc
  • Human-in-the-loop as the 2026 norm: agents generate and stage, humans approve before publication; approval stays mandatory for budget changes, sensitive copy and client-facing conclusions; Gartner: 40 % of enterprise apps will include task-specific agents by end-2026. A Improvado, Birdeye, getclaw
  • EU AI Act, Article 50 (applies from 2 August 2026; machine-readable marking of generated text/images by 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market): AI chatbots must disclose they are AI; generated content must be marked. P European Commission guidelines, Orrick summary

Reading. "Optional AI support" (D6) is right on the evidence: the machine is rules and integrations first; AI drafts where switched on, is labelled as AI where the law requires, and never sends without the owner. The videos' rule and the regulation agree.

8. First market: New York (United States)

  • CAN-SPAM requires no prior consent for commercial e-mail — B2B or B2C — but every message needs a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days, a physical address, accurate headers and no deceptive subject lines. P FTC compliance guide, ANA FAQ
  • TCPA: marketing SMS needs prior express written consent (signed, with clear disclosures; the FCC's 2025 one-to-one rule was vacated and the prior standard reinstated on 29 August 2025); opt-outs honoured within 10 business days from April 2025; statutory damages $500–1 500 per message. P/A FCC document, Nelson Mullins, BCLP on the opt-out rules

Reading. Provider activation by e-mail (130 providers have an address on their card) is lawful without consent; every family-facing SMS needs written consent captured on the platform, and the frames show that as a state, not an assumption. E-mail and push carry the digests; SMS only where a parent opted in for a specific provider.

8b. Reference market: Hungary

  • B2B e-mail to corporate addresses does not require prior consent in Hungary under Act XLVIII of 2008 — unless the address contains personal data (a named person's address does); B2C requires express prior consent. P DLA Piper: electronic marketing, Hungary, CMS guide
  • 57.4 % of Hungarian SMEs have at least basic digital intensity (EU 72.9 %); AI use lags "in particular for SMEs, due to a lack of digital skills". P Digital Decade 2025 country report, Hungary

Reading. For the Hungarian reference instance the same activation is lawful to info@-type addresses and not to a named owner without consent; the digital-skills gap is the reason the machine must run with "nobody logs in" on any instance.

9. What the two reference videos add (owner-supplied, 2026-09-19)

Not evidence — a target shape, read from 42 extracted frames (02-audit.md §3): departments as the unit of automation; a brand knowledge layer as plain files (voice-guide.md, positioning.md, offer.md, leads.md); an orchestrator; an access control plane; reviews and reputation answered first; a market radar that turns a competitor's launch into a strategist's note; a daily recap; and the operating rule the whole system obeys — it drafts, it can't post, it stops and asks, the owner still owns, everything else runs.

10. What was not found

No public figure on claimed-to-paid conversion for any listing platform (Yelp's annual reports do not break it out); no Hungarian fitness-membership statistics beyond the European total; no independent study of AI-agent marketing outcomes for one-person businesses — the ROI figures circulating are vendor claims and are not used here.
