business.direct — design system

Every value on this page is read live from assets/tokens.css. The role names are DiscountDirect's GDS 6.5.0 names (D5); the product owns one accent, teal, where DiscountDirect is indigo (D8). The components below are the ones the three views are composed from. Approval of this page fixes colour, type, spacing and these components; layouts come after, as two frames.

What approval means: nothing arranged here is a layout. It says "these are the parts, in these colours, at these sizes". Say what to change and the page changes before any frame is drawn.

1 · Colour

Four status colours carry the machine's states everywhere: ok (sent, live, connected), wait (awaiting the owner), off (paused, not connected), danger. The accent is used for one thing: what business.direct itself does.
