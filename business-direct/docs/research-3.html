business.direct — research III: data-driven marketing decisions — case studies and best practices

Third research round, 2026-09-19, on the owner's ask: "the industry research for it, to have the best case studies and best practices as a source of our services" — it being the data-driven decision flow of D26 (16-analytics-and-unit-economics.md). Every figure links to where it was read; P = primary (filing, the company's own release, the report itself), A = aggregator or trade article. §6 maps each finding to a business.direct service.

1. What the incumbents in our category actually measure and sell

Yelp (local services, US — the closest public comparable). - 496,000 paying advertising locations at Q4 2025 (−5 % year over year) while average revenue per location set an annual record; Services advertising revenue $948 million (+8 %); Request-a-Quote projects +15 % excluding paid search, "driven by improvements to the flow and increased adoption of Yelp Assistant". P Yelp Q4 2025 letter to shareholders, Yelp press release, 12 Feb 2026 Reading: the growth lever moved from more advertisers to more revenue per advertiser and more projects per advertiser — ARPA and delivered value, our §2.3 and §3.1. - Yelp Assistant project submissions +400 % year over year in Q2 2025; the business owner's inbox shows AI summaries of each job. P Yelp fall 2025 release, Yelp spring 2025 release - Yelp Receptionist (from $99/month) and Yelp Host (from $149/month), October 2025: answers calls, qualifies leads, collects quote details, transcribes and summarises. P Yelp blog, 21 Oct 2025 This is our missed-call text-back and after-hours voice (P9), priced by the incumbent. - Hatch acquisition, 21 January 2026, $270 million cash + $30 million retention: an AI lead-management and customer-communication platform, $25 million ARR growing 70 %; Yelp's words — Hatch is "solving challenging lead management and communication pain points for services businesses" to "help service providers operate and grow more efficiently". P Yelp IR. Hatch's predictive lead scoring lets media buying reallocate budget in real time toward channels that historically delivered higher-scoring leads. A PPC Land The closest public statement of our thesis: the platform buys the provider's one-person sales team and the lead-scoring loop that decides where the next dollar goes.

Angi (home services leads, US). - Pros buy full-priced leads within a monthly budget, discounted leads in a subscription, double-opt-in leads à la carte, and pre-priced offerings; 2025: 15,543 thousand service requests, 20,195 thousand leads, proprietary-channel revenue +17 %; revenue per lead +11 % in Q3 2025 after the sales force was consolidated (mix moved away from discounted pros), −2 % in Q4 when subscription pros were sent leads above contract value. P Angi 10-K FY2025, Angi Q3 2025 release, Angi Q1 2025 metrics Reading: a lead marketplace manages two numbers — revenue per lead and lead quality — and reports both; ours are "families delivered" and "trials → enrolment".

Thumbtack — $400 million+ revenue growing 27 %, 300,000+ pros, distribution inside ChatGPT and Claude (research II §2.1). A Contrary Research, Stanford GSB case

Rover (pet care marketplace) — gross booking value past $1 billion in 2024; "Perfect Match" matching rollout credited with ~12 % lift in booking conversion and ~9 % higher retention; an added service line raised customer LTV ~20 % in two years. A Brineweb, Vocal (secondary figures; treat as indicative).

2. Unit-economics best practice (the rules behind the economics screen)

  • LTV : CAC of 3 : 1 is the accepted health line; below 1 : 1 is unsustainable, above 5 : 1 signals under-investment. Sales-led SMB companies target 6–12-month payback at 3 : 1. A PM Toolkit, SaaS Hero
  • CAC payback: median B2B SaaS 15–16 months; top quartile 6–8; bottom quartile 24–36; Bessemer's targets by segment — under 12 months for SMB, 18 mid-market, 24 enterprise. A Aleph, Optifai, 939 companies, Foundry CRO
  • The metrics set: a16z's 16 startup metrics (CAC, LTV, ARPA, churn, activation, retention …) and 13 marketplace metrics (GMV, take rate, liquidity, supply/demand concentration). P a16z: 16 startup metrics, a16z: 13 marketplace metrics, 16 more; A Lenny's marketplace metrics
  • Cohorts, not averages: LTV and churn by acquisition month and by channel, so that a CAC is compared with the LTV of the cohort it bought. A DualEntry

Reading. The economics screen's 3 : 1 badge and the 12-month payback line are the industry's lines, not ours; the plan's next step is cohorts by acquisition channel (touch · call · content) once events exist.

3. Measurement best practice: attribution, incrementality, mix modelling

  • Triangulate three methods: attribution for the day-to-day, incrementality tests for causal proof, marketing-mix modelling for the budget. A Measured, FELD M
  • Open-source MMM exists and is free: Google Meridian (Bayesian, with GeoX geo-incrementality announced May 2026), Meta Robyn, PyMC-Marketing. P Google Meridian; A Improvado, Elevarus on GeoX
  • But not yet for us: under ~2 years of data or under ~$1 million annual spend the sample is too small for stable coefficients — start with last-touch attribution plus periodic incrementality tests (geo holdouts, PSA controls) and revisit MMM in 12–18 months. A Digital Applied
  • Marketplace experiments interfere with themselves (a treated listing takes demand from a control listing); Airbnb's answer is cluster randomisation — by neighbourhood, not by listing. P Management Science, Airbnb pricing meta-experiment, Airbnb engineering: Experiments at Airbnb

Reading. Our attribution (last touch, 7 days for a sign-up, 30 for a booking — analytics §6) is the right first stage. The first incrementality test business.direct can run is a neighbourhood holdout: run the content engine in Park Slope and not in Carroll Gardens for four weeks and compare sign-ups — cluster by neighbourhood exactly as Airbnb does. MMM waits.

4. Decision best practice: experimentation and next-best-action

  • Booking.com runs ~25,000 experiments a year; anyone can test without permission; the culture, not the tooling, is the asset (Stefan Thomke, HBS). P HBR, March 2020, HBR IdeaCast 2024; A VWO
  • Next-best-action: lifecycle teams replace segment campaigns with a per-customer decision (which action, which channel, which moment) driven by propensity scores (convert, churn, upgrade) and continuous 1 : 1 experiments. A Hightouch, Braze, CleverTap
  • Lead scoring reallocates spend: Hatch's model (now Yelp's) scores each lead and moves budget toward the channels that produced high scores. A PPC Land

Reading. Our "next dollar" (analytics §4) is next-best-action at the budget level; the provider-level version is a propensity to claim / to upgrade per provider (phone on card, trial policy, next session, replies) that orders the sequence and the call list — one score, computed nightly from the same events.

5. What the case studies say about the provider's own numbers

  • Speed to lead, reminders and reply time (research I §2–3) are the numbers Yelp now sells back to the provider as Receptionist and Hatch; Angi prices leads by quality.
  • A provider's dashboard therefore needs three lines, in this order: enquiries answered and how fast, trials → enrolments and their value, what the plan costs — the "your return" line built in round 5.

8. What was not found

  • Yelp and Angi do not disclose CAC or advertiser churn; revenue per location / per lead and paying-location counts are the public proxies.
  • Rover's "Perfect Match" and LTV figures come from secondary write-ups, not a filing.
  • No public case study of a youth-activities platform's unit economics exists; the home-services comparables are the nearest.
