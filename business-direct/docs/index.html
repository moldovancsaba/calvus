business.direct — project documentation

An automated marketing machine for B2B2C listing platforms — the one-person sales and marketing team for a listed business, run by the platform, with the consumer as the third role. First client: Your Field NYC (getyourfield.com). Sibling of DiscountDirect. Built to the prototyping standard (PROTOTYPING.md); this index is the process log.
