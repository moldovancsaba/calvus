business.direct — project documentation

business.direct is a standalone product for classified media owners — listing platforms, directories, marketplaces of local businesses — that runs the three jobs their business never stops needing: marketing (content generated from the listings and delivered to every medium), B2B sales (every listed business contacted and acquired as a paying advertiser) and retention (churn seen weeks ahead), with a human gate on everything and the owner's own economics as a service. Example persona and first buyer: ClassScout, operator of Your Field NYC; the prototype runs on their real listings. Sibling of DiscountDirect. This index is also the process log.
