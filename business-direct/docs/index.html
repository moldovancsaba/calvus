business.direct — project documentation

This documentation set is being replaced (audit D40). business.direct is a standalone product — the one-person sales and marketing team; ClassScout is its first customer. The presentation, the executive summary and the business case are written for the product; most other pages still read as the first instance's project until the transformation programme replaces them.

An automated marketing machine for B2B2C listing platforms — the one-person sales and marketing team for a listed business, run by the platform, with the consumer as the third role. First client: ClassScout, operator of Your Field NYC (getyourfield.com); built for any client, each with its own responsible-data policy record. Sibling of DiscountDirect. Built to the prototyping standard (PROTOTYPING.md); this index is the process log.
